Jak Project Highlights

Our portfolio represents the entirety of our capabilities, ranging from strategic digital roadmaps to technology implementations and process optimisations. Each project highlights our collaborative approach, innovative solutions, and the measurable impact we have had on our clients’ businesses.

Jak Telecoms

We partnered with Jak Telecoms to enhance communication infrastructure through innovative software solutions. Overcoming legacy system limitations, we developed scalable and secure software tailored to their needs. Leveraging cloud technologies and mobile app development, we improved connectivity and user experience. Our work resulted in enhanced network reliability, scalability, and positive user feedback, reflecting our commitment to innovative software solutions

Duke Consult

We partnered with Duke Consult to develop a robust online platform connecting students with study partners and providing educational services. Our team at Jak Technologies crafted a user-friendly website that enhances student engagement and facilitates seamless international relocations. Leveraging our expertise in software development and technology, we enabled Duke Consult to expand their reach and empower students globally through innovative digital solutions.

Auto Invento

Auto Invento is an innovative app developed by Jak Technologies to streamline automotive inventory management for dealerships and car rental businesses. Our mobile solution simplifies inventory tracking, vehicle information management, and customer interactions. By leveraging our expertise in software development, we delivered Auto Invento to enhance operational efficiency and customer satisfaction within the automotive industry.

Eat Express

Eat Express, developed by Jak Technologies, is a pioneering app that redefines food delivery by connecting users with local restaurants. Our platform offers a seamless ordering experience with real-time tracking and efficient delivery logistics. With Eat Express, we leverage our software expertise to enhance customer satisfaction and convenience in the food delivery industry.

Ez Exchange

Eze Exchange, developed by Jak Technologies, is a user-friendly app designed to simplify cryptocurrency trading. Our platform provides secure and intuitive features for buying, selling, and trading digital assets. With real-time market insights and robust security measures, Eze Exchange empowers users to participate confidently in the cryptocurrency market

Hotel Hub

Hotel Hub, developed by Jak Technologies, is a user-friendly app designed to streamline hotel bookings for travelers. Our platform offers a seamless experience for discovering and reserving accommodations worldwide. With intuitive navigation and flexible booking options, Hotel Hub ensures convenience and satisfaction for users seeking memorable travel experiences.
