Jak Technologies ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website Jaktechhub.com (the "Website"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of cookies on our Website.


What are Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of text sent by your web browser by a website you visit. A cookie file is stored in your web browser and allows the Website or a third-party service to recognize you and make your next visit easier and the Website more useful to you.


How We Use Cookies

When you use and access our Website, we may place a number of cookies files in your web browser. We use cookies for the following purposes:

1. Authentication: We use cookies to identify and authenticate users and to remember user preferences.

2. Security: Cookies help detect security incidents, protect against malicious activity, and ensure the security of our services.

3. Preferences: We use cookies to remember your settings and preferences, such as language preferences.

4. Analytics: We use cookies to gather analytics data to improve the performance and usability of our Website.

5. Marketing: We may use cookies for marketing purposes, including remarketing and targeted advertising.


Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the Website, deliver advertisements on and through the Website, and so on.


Your Choices Regarding Cookies

You have the option to either accept or refuse these cookies and to know when a cookie is being sent to your computer. If you choose to refuse our cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Website.


Changes to Our Cookies Policy

We may update our Cookies Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Cookies Policy on this page.
